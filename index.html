About us

What we do

Director's Choice Ltd is a one-stop merchandise partner for theme parks and IP owners. Plush, silicone, stainless steel drinkware, mobile accessories — developed, produced and delivered under one roof.

Who we help

Since 2011 we have built merchandise for Disney, Universal Studios, SeaWorld, Kennedy Space Center and other premier destinations worldwide.

How we help

Seasoned project managers guide your product from the first concept sketch all the way to shelf placement. One team, one schedule, one point of contact. You always know where your programme stands and what comes next.

Why it's different

Our team brings more than a century of combined experience in theme park merchandise — the kind of judgement that turns a rough sketch into a finished product that holds up in a park store. Behind us stands a network of top-tier production partners, proven on the most demanding programmes and inspected throughout the run, not just at the end. And we bring hands-on experience with legal compliance across different markets, so requirements are designed in from the start rather than discovered late.

The Director's Choice advantage

What truly distinguishes us is not only what we make, but how we work alongside our clients every day.

i

Routine in-line inspection with transparent reporting

We don't wait until the end of production to verify quality. Our quality teams conduct routine in-line inspections throughout the manufacturing process and report progress and findings to clients on a regular, scheduled basis — so you always know exactly where your order stands, what has been verified, and what to expect next.

ii

12-hour response guarantee

We guarantee a response to every client inquiry within 12 hours, regardless of time zone or complexity. Whether it's a quick clarification, an urgent design revision, or a sourcing question, you will hear from us the same business day — because in the theme park industry, momentum matters.

Built over years of direct collaboration with world premium IP and theme parks.

Proven trust

Earned through consistent performance against the industry's highest standards.
